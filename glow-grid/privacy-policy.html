Glow Grid Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · App: Glow Grid (com.viralapps.glowgrid) · Developer: ViralApps

This policy explains what information Glow Grid ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact us any time at brannstrom9911@gmail.com.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your best score, coins, powerups, settings and the game in progress are saved on your device so you can continue later. We do not receive this data unless you use the optional online features described below. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Optional online features (Friends & challenges)

If you open Friends and choose a display name, we create an anonymous account for you. We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name. We then store:

DataWhy
A random account IDTo recognise your device's account
Your display name and a friend codeSo friends can find and recognise you
Your best scoreFor the friends leaderboard and challenges
Your friends list and challenge resultsTo run friend challenges

Only you and the friends you add can see your name, best score and challenge results. This data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which processes it for us. Like any server, Supabase may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, ads between games, and optional rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location
  • ad interactions
  • diagnostic information

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, we ask for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with ☰ Menu → Privacy settings, when that option is shown in your region. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't use your game data to build advertising profiles. Google AdMob handles ads under its own policies, as described above.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Online data is kept until you delete it. To delete it:

  • In the app: open Friends and tap Delete my online profile. Your account, name, friend code, friends list and challenges are permanently removed from our server right away.
  • By email: if you no longer have the app, email brannstrom9911@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glow Grid data" and your friend code or display name. We'll delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Children

Glow Grid isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the online features, contact us and we'll delete the data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
